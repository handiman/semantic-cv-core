# Code of Conduct

Semantic-CV is a small project, and I would like it to be a friendly place to take part in, whoever you are and whatever your level of experience.

## What is expected

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Keep discussions about the work, not the person.
- Give and take feedback gracefully. Disagreeing is fine; being rude about it is not.
- Help newcomers find their way.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where this applies

In the project's repositories (issues, pull requests, discussions and commits), and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, email [security@semantic.cv](mailto:security@semantic.cv). Reports are read by the maintainer and handled in confidence.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and other contributions that break this code, and may block anyone from the project, temporarily or permanently.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
